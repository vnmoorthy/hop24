# Code of Conduct

Hop 24 follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. The short version:

- Be welcoming and respectful to everyone, regardless of experience, identity or background.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person. Honest numbers beat hype; so does honest review.
- No harassment, insults, trolling, or publishing others' private information.
- Disagree in the open, assume good faith, and move on when a decision is made.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent the project.
Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions that do not follow it, and may ask people
who repeatedly break it to leave.

To report a problem, open a GitHub issue at https://github.com/vnmoorthy/hop24/issues or, if it
is not something to post publicly, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports
are handled with discretion.

The full text, including the enforcement guidelines, is at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
