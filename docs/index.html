Hop 24

Cross a real highway. When a truck gets you, the archive finds it on every other camera.

Hop 24 is a video agent disguised as an arcade game, built in one day at the VAST Builders Challenge (Team 10). You play a chicken crossing a real 30-second I-24 Nashville clip; every collision comes from the YOLO11 detections the DataEngine pipeline already stored in VastDB, tested against FastSAM vehicle silhouettes. When a vehicle hits you, the agent reads the segment's stored Cosmos description, runs one hybrid search across every highway camera in the archive, files an incident report with the evidence frame and the exact VastDB SQL, and can send the clip back through the pipeline so the next search is sharper. An autopilot trained with Q-learning on the archive's own detections, tracked in Weights & Biases, crosses on its own.
